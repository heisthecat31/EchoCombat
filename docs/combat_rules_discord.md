#  Echo Combat: Private Match RULES
Combat private matches now have a **RULES** screen on the podium terminal (like Arena's). Hit **RULES**, change values with **- / +**, and use **NEXT** to get to page 2. Every client sees the server's values.
-# Needs the EchoCombat mod on the server. Players without it just get stock Combat.

##  Page 1: The Point
- **POINT UNLOCK**: seconds before the point unlocks. The "point unlocking in" call and the 5..1 countdown follow it.
- **CAPTURE TIME**: seconds to fill the capture bar. **0 = instant capture.**
- **BLUE SCORE / ORANGE SCORE**: starting points for each team (75 wins).

##  Page 2: Match Rules
- **LOADOUTS: ALL / CLASSIC / NEW**
 • **ALL**: every gun, tac mod and ordnance
 • **CLASSIC**: only the stock 12 (Pulsar, Nova, Comet, Meteor / Repair Matrix, Threat Scanner, Energy Barrier, Phase Shift / Detonator, Stun Field, Arc Mine, Instant Repair)
 • **NEW**: only the new guns (Protostar, Quark, Blazar, Starburst), plus every tac mod and ordnance
 • Players on banned gear get swapped automatically
- **SERIES-10 ONLY: ON / OFF**: everyone's chassis shows as the Series-10 body
- **POINT RATE**: how fast a held point scores: 0.25x up to 2.0x in 0.25 steps, then 2.5x

## ⏸ PAUSE
Once both teams are ready, **TEAM READY** turns into **PAUSE / UNPAUSE**. Anyone can press it.
- **PAUSE**: everyone goes back to the war room, guns, tac mods and ordnances are disabled, points and capture freeze, the point goes neutral, the round clock and point-unlock timer stop, and the pre-match orbs come back
- **UNPAUSE**: the announcer counts down **5, 4, 3, 2, 1**. Orbs work again so you can spawn back in, but guns stay off and the point stays frozen until 0
- Pressing it during the countdown pauses again

## 🚚 Payload maps (Fission, Gauss)
RULES is a single page: **LOADOUTS**, **SERIES-10 ONLY** and **PAYLOAD SPEED** (0.25x up to 2.5x, how fast George moves when pushed). The capture point rows aren't shown.
- **PAUSE** works straight away if George hasn't been pushed yet this round, or while he's held at a checkpoint
- Once he's moving, PAUSE shows **QUEUED** and kicks in at the next checkpoint
- George stays still while paused (testing)
